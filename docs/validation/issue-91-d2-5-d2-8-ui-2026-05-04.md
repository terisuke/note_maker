  `#brief-result #brief-card`.

  a dedicated transcript log with status text, focus target, and composer
  affordance.

  focus restore behavior.

  hidden behind the topbar trigger on first load.

  mobile drawer layout, and tab selected-state updates without changing the
  Playwright-visible button roles.

- Added `role="dialog"` semantics and Tab/Shift+Tab focus trap for the mobile
  settings drawer.
- Wrapped `#preview-content` in the right-rail draft preview card and added a
  mobile artifacts-rail toggle.
- During an active interview, the right rail now shows a live article brief
  card and a draft outline preview before final brief completion or draft
  generation.
- Mirrored transcript and right-rail artifact summaries into
  `Alpine.store('app')` as a D2 migration bridge.
